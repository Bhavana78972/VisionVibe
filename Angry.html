60 Seconds to Reset 🧘

Take one minute to try a fun, distracting exercise. Stand up and try this:

  • The T-Rex Wiggle: Tense your hands into tiny T-Rex arms and wiggle your entire body vigorously for 15 seconds.
  • The Silent Scream: Take a deep breath in, hold it, and let out a huge, silent scream (or a gentle hum) as you exhale for 10 seconds.
  • The Confused Chicken: Flap your arms slowly, lift one knee, and tilt your head for 15 seconds.

Why this helps: Engaging your body and finding humor breaks the mental loop of anger!
